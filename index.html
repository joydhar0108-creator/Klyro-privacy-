Klyro Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Klyro ("the app", "we") is an all-in-one utility app with PDF tools, a calculator, a photo scanner and converters. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not.

1. Your files stay on your device

Klyro works offline. When you view, merge, split, edit, compress or annotate a PDF, scan a page with the camera, or convert a file, all of that processing happens on your phone. We do not upload your files, photos, scans or documents to any server, and we cannot see them.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, sign-in, name, email or phone number.
  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves.
  • We do not run our own servers for the app.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only to scan documents when you choose "Scan with camera". Photos are processed on your device.
  • Photos / files: only the files you pick yourself, using Android's file and photo pickers.
  • Internet: only to show advertising (see below). The tools work without internet.

4. Data stored on your device

Klyro keeps small settings on your device, such as your favourite tools, a short activity history, and pages you added to an unfinished scan. This data never leaves your phone. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Klyro shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain information to show and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on where you live, you may see a consent message asking for your choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing and saving files

When you tap Save or Share, Klyro hands the file to the Android share sheet. What happens next depends on the app or service you choose, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

7. Children

Klyro is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Because your files are processed on your device and are not sent to us, they are protected by your phone's own security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
